Meet Owefolk: the shared-expense app built for friends, not spreadsheets. Sign in securely with Firebase, create a group, and see exactly where everyone stands. Scan a receipt with private on-device OCR to fill the merchant and total, then review it before saving. Split a dinner, rental, or trip equally, by exact amounts, or by percentage, always down to the penny. When it is time to settle, Owefolk opens Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, Zelle, or another payment method. Owefolk never pretends to move money. The payer marks it sent, and the recipient confirms it. Every update stays visible in a clear activity history. Privacy controls, consent-aware ads, encrypted deletion requests, analytics, performance monitoring, and crash reporting are built in. Owefolk: fair math, clear friendships.
