Account controls

Delete your Owefolk account.

The fastest path is Profile → Delete account in the Android app. If you cannot access the app, submit this encrypted request.

Your email and details are encrypted before storage. The public service never stores them in plaintext.

What happens next

After ownership verification, personal profile data, authentication credentials, device tokens, and payment handles are deleted. Shared financial history is anonymized where it must remain understandable to other group members.
