Features

Everything a fair split needs

Settling up should be a one-tap goodbye, not a spreadsheet argument.

📷

Scan the receipt

Take a photo and let private on-device recognition suggest the merchant and total for you to review.

🧾

Split it fairly

Choose equal, exact, or percentage shares. Owefolk keeps every cent accounted for.

👥

See who owes whom

Direct balances or simplified repayments — the group sees where it stands at a glance.

🤝

Pay their way

See your friend's preferred provider and handle, then open the right payment destination.

📋

Shared ledger

A timestamped history every member can read, so "I paid that" never needs to be a fight.

🔒

Privacy first

Receipts never leave your phone. Diagnostics can be switched off from your profile.
